OUR AREAS

Designed to provoke admiration, tempt and seduce on the very sand of Atlanterra Beach, focused with a single purpose: to please you.

The perfect fusion of luxury and privacy in an exclusive environment, Varadero Zahara Beach&Hotel in the heart of Atlanterra Beach has 17 rooms with exquisite Balinese decoration. We put at your disposal the Vambú restaurant located in a tropical garden which is connected to The Beach&Club on the beach and The Velvet Garden’s, a garden area where you can make both the Welcome drink and later celebration in a natural and sensual environment for those who are looking for weddings integrated in a single space and a magnificent team of professionals.

Maximum capacity 170 guests, a unique space in Zahara for those who bet on insurance. If you are looking for a private WEDDING, Varadero is the perfect place. A delicate service to spend the most unforgettable days of your life in an idyllic setting surrounded by nature. A beachfront hotel that will leave no one indifferent.

Our Team

Hotel Varadero has an events department that is responsible for organising weddings and catering, we do a careful job as a wedding planner with extensive knowledge in decoration, gastronomy, oenology and reception of guests, both for individuals and companies. An exclusive wedding in an open space just for you and your friends… for those who want it all and with the most personalised attention. Pure pampering…

We won’t tuck you into bed at the end of the day (we prefer you to choose who to do it with) but we can take care of preparing the best events for you. “Say YES at the most sensual hotel in Atlanterra, fill yourselves with optimism and love each other”. The Varadero Team will do the rest.

Passionate about our work, we will make your stay as comfortable and pleasant as possible. We want you to feel at home. Rest assured and trust in the team of professionals that will make your wedding the hit of all times……
