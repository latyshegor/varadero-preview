VARADERO · OUR PHILOSOPHY

Varadero Mission, Vision and Philosophy.

To be the best choice as a destination for relaxation, pleasure and delight of the senses, providing unrivalled quality facilities and service within the Zahara territory. Belonging to this brand implies not only unique facilities, an unbeatable location and an attentive and personalised service; it also implies exceeding the expectations of our guests, who expect to live a dream experience and unforgettable moments. Values – We are a hotel specialising in treatment and service excellence. We provide the necessary space and atmosphere to spend unrepeatable moments, we work for the wellbeing of our guests and we live to be better every day. Objective Varadero – Our main objective is to position ourselves as one of the best hotels in Zahara, fulfilling every day the total satisfaction of our guests. Meeting day by day the total satisfaction of each one of our guests and clients creating a unique experience within the most demanding quality standards.

Facilities

The hotel has 17 spacious rooms designed and equipped with all the comforts where every detail has been taken care of to make your stay comfortable.

We also have the VAMBÚ restaurant, an idyllic setting surrounded by nature, an original proposal for those who enjoy elegant yet exotic atmospheres. A fusion of art, creativity, tradition and a homemade touch are the ingredients to make every meal more than a dream.

In addition to all this, we have a Beach&Club at the foot of the beach with a tapas bar and delicious signature cocktails, where you can enjoy a delicious meal or drink at any time of the day.
